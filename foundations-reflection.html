✩Foundations Reflections 26/09/2025✩

What are three new things you have learnt about yourself and your ego due to the Human Skills learning?

I can be persistent in a way that is occasionally detrimental and I need to remember to take space from problems sometimes, I am overall confident in my emotional intellect and I enjoy engaging with others even if it is outside of settings where I would typically feel most comfortable doing so.

What are the role of values, empathy, and self-awareness in learning and programming?

Having strong values and empathy is important to both the person on person interactive parts of programming like working with other programmers and a lot of the necessary considerations when creating software that people are going to interact and engage with, like accounting for accessibility and a wide range of human experiences and needs.

What has surprised you the most about the Human Skills learning?

How much of the curriculum was dedicated to it.

What were the most challenging aspects of the Human Skills learning?

Writing answers to prompts and questions that actually convey complex things that I feel about given topics.

Why do you think we, a programming school, are spending so much time focusing on Human Skills learning in a web development Bootcamp course?

Because of how important those skills are to being a part of a team or workplace and how they can affect your process and the way you make things.

Does the time you spent studying Human Skills learning here feel like a waste of time? Should you have just used that time to practise programming instead? Justify your answer.

I don’t think it was a waste to do the human skills portion of the course. Whilst I do think that most of the topics covered were pretty basic things that any balanced and healthy person would already understand and engage with, I found it useful to review them within the context of programming and its surrounding industry and environment.
