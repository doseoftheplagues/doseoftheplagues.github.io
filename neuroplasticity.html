✩Neuroplasticity + Growth Mindset 16/09/2025✩

How does understanding the principles of neuroplasticity benefit people?

Having a good understanding of the way that pathways in the brain can be formed, shaped and reinforced could be invaluable to someone’s ability to learn and change

How might you engage with the principles of neuroplasticity for your benefit?

Knowing that pathways in the brain can be formed through repetition and time allows us to form an approach to learning that applies the principles of neuroplasticity in a positive way. Relating learning to personal experiences or applicability to your own life, learning repetitively whilst approaching topics from different directions and providing yourself with challenging situations within your learning all ways you can reinforce and forge new neural pathways to benefit your learning.

What are some of the ways to increase your neuroplasticity?

You can increase neuroplasticity by engaging with topics and activities that are new and different to you, breaking out of the typical pathways and patterns your brain has become accustomed to, challenging yourself in new ways and forming new pathways around things that are foreign to you.

Neuroplasticity in Clinical Practice

What is a growth mindset?

A growth mindset is an understanding that progress and learning are non fixed non linear processes that require time and a variety of approaches to reach desired goals. The opposite of a growth mindset would be a fixed perception of learning and progress, where someone believes that their intelligence and ability to improve are static and unable to change.

How will you integrate a growth mindset into your learning journey?

I integrate a growth mindset into my learning by reminding myself that challenging moments in my learning journey are as valuable if not more than those that are easy and take little time. Having an understanding that needing to approach a task a multitude of times or in different ways greatly improves my ability to adapt to challenges and grow my skills and emotional resilience to difficulty. I maintain positivity and hope for improvement even at moments that seem to be dead ends.

How will these understandings shape or influence your learning plan and strategy?

I feel that I have already been employing this knowledge and strategies based around neuroplasticity and growth subconsciously, but perhaps from now on I will consider them in a more conscious/mindful way when I am approaching my learning.
