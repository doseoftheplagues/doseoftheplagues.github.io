What is Emotional Intelligence?

Emotional intelligence is the ability to deeply understand, engage with and regulate your own emotions. People with high emotional intelligence are able to empathise and connect with the emotions others experience.

How is EQ different to IQ?

Emotional intelligence deals with empathy and the often complex emotions that humans experience. IQ stands for Intelligence Quotient and refers to general human intelligence, factoring in such things as problem solving, memory and logical reasoning. Neither term refers to something concrete or measurable, despite IQ being often misconceived as being so. Much of the history of IQ testing is rooted in eugenics and has been an instrument in reinforcing of white supremacy and other systems of oppression. Attempted measurements of IQ are often narrow and do not capture the complexity and range of human intelligence and experience.

The Eugenic Origins of IQ Testing - Ajitha Reddy

Why is EQ important?

Being able to understand and manage one's own emotions as well as engage and empathise with the emotions of others is a fundamental life skill. Humans depend on social connections and having the emotional intelligence necessary to establish meaningful emotional connections is immeasurably valuable in interpersonal relationships, workplaces and in everyday life.
