✩Te whare tapa wha blog 16/09/2025✩

How do you maintain Taha Tinana (Physical Wellbeing)?

I exercise regularly from home, usually at random times working around my school schedule or when I am in need of a break from coding.

How do you maintain Taha Whānau (Family Wellbeing)?

I maintain my social circle and make time for the people I am close to on a regular basis. I often attend communal meals with friends of mine.

How do you maintain Taha Hinengaro (Emotional and Mental Wellbeing)?

I try my best to maintain a balance between all of my interests ( technology, music, live performance, etc ) and that balance helps to maintain my mental wellbeing.

How do you maintain Wairua (Spiritual Wellbeing)?

I find that my Spiritual wellbeing is connected to my connections to the land and my enviroment and is maintained when those connections are also.

How do you maintain Whenua (Interconnections to the land and environment)?

I try to spend regular periods of time outside, walking to places I need to be and avoiding using my car or public transport when that is a realistic option.
